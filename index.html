From kitchen to plate

How Kamochi is made.

Six simple steps for preparing the snack.

Wash

Wash the sweet potatoes thoroughly.

Boil

Boil until soft enough to mash.

Mash

Peel and mash the cooked sweet potatoes in a clean bowl.

Mix

Add cheese and mix the filling well.

Wrap

Place the filling in each wrapper, roll, and seal the edges.

Fry

With adult supervision, fry in hot oil until golden. Drain before serving.
